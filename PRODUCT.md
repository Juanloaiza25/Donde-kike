# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Vinext/React through OpenAI Sites.

## Users

People choosing a fast, indulgent meal on mobile or desktop, with the immediate job of deciding what to order.

## Product Purpose

Present an original fast-food restaurant whose main attraction is its hamburgers and move visitors directly toward the menu.

## Positioning

The product centers a flame-seared double smash burger and a bold Spanish voice instead of an interchangeable fast-food catalog.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Single-page persuasive website in Spanish. The burger dominates the first viewport. Commercial claims and unverified operational details are intentionally absent.

## Brand Commitments

Working brand: Brasa Brava. Tone: direct, appetizing, energetic.

## Evidence on Hand

Original generated hero photograph at `public/hero-burger.png`. No verified testimonials, locations, delivery partners, or statistics.

## Product Principles

- Show the food before explaining the brand.
- Make the next action obvious within seconds.
- Use concrete menu language instead of generic promises.
- Never fabricate proof or operational claims.
