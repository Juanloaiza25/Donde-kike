# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Vinext/React through OpenAI Sites.

## Users

People choosing a fast, indulgent meal on mobile or desktop, with the immediate job of deciding what to order.

## Product Purpose

Present the real fast-food restaurant Donde Kike, with hamburgers as its main attraction, and move visitors directly toward a categorized menu and WhatsApp ordering.

## Positioning

The product leads with a generous double burger and a bold Spanish voice, then makes the restaurant's unusually broad menu easy to scan by category.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Single-page persuasive website in Spanish. The burger dominates the first viewport. Visitors can combine products and quantities in a local cart, then send the full order through WhatsApp. Menu prices are not published because they are not legible in the supplied source photos; visitors are directed to WhatsApp for current prices and promotions.

## Brand Commitments

Brand: Donde Kike. Tone: direct, appetizing, energetic and familiar.

## Evidence on Hand

Original generated hero photograph at `public/hero-burger.png`. Two supplied photographs document the menu categories and products. Verified contact numbers shown in those photographs: WhatsApp 302 326 6030 and domicilios 301 352 5168. No verified testimonials, street address, delivery partners, statistics or legible prices.

## Product Principles

- Show the food before explaining the brand.
- Make the next action obvious within seconds.
- Use concrete menu language instead of generic promises.
- Never fabricate proof or operational claims.
