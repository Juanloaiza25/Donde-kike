# Quality Bar

- Product image remains the most visually dominant object at desktop and mobile.
- A first-time visitor can identify the restaurant, featured burger and primary action within one viewport.
- Display typography never exceeds 6rem or collides with adjacent copy.
- Menu grouping uses rules and spacing, not cards, labels or decorative numbering.
- Every interactive element has keyboard-visible focus and press feedback.
- Desktop and 390px mobile remain free of horizontal overflow.
- Color, selection, scrollbar, focus and reduced-motion states belong to the same palette.
