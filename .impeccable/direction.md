# Brasa Brava Direction Contract

FORM seed key: `469d277f`

## World

A neighborhood burger counter translated into flat sign-paint fields: tomato red, deli-paper cream, mustard yellow and near-black ink. Product photography supplies texture; the interface stays typographic and direct.

## First viewport

One double smash burger occupies the full field at life-size scale. The Spanish product name and one mustard order action sit in the remaining negative space. No feature cards, claims, or preliminary explanation.

## Visitor path

Appetite → three-item menu → preparation point of view → provisional opening status → contact action.

## Signature interaction

Mobile navigation opens as a full mustard field in 180ms with exponential ease-out, starts already visible at 0.98 scale/opacity, and closes faster. All pressables use 0.97 active scale; reduced motion removes movement.

## Responsive contract

Desktop composes photo and copy side by side. Mobile crops the burger above the decision copy, converts menu rows to vertical ordering, and stacks manifesto and location content without horizontal overflow.

## Honest risk

The blunt palette and large type can overpower the food if copy expands. Display text is capped at 6rem and operational claims remain absent until verified.
